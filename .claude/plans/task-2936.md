# #2936 — enable `crossModelReview` on Arbiter

The owner consented on 2026-09-26 (#2775) to diff egress to OpenAI for Arbiter only. Turn on
`crossModelReview` in Arbiter's own `arbiter.json`, every field explicit, so `ship --review-round`
dispatches the Codex seat natively. AC-3 uses option B: the `E_REVIEW_NO_DATA` refusal names the
independent-verifier fallback.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-2936.1: `arbiter.json` carries `crossModelReview` with `enabled: true` and `diffEgressConsent: true`, and passes schema validation. Every other field is set explicitly (providers, slots, timeout, onUnavailable, model, effort), none left to defaults.
- AC-2936.2: On a live task, `ship --review-round` dispatches the Codex seat and records its envelope. The review completes with no hand-written envelope, including when `.git` is read-only in the Codex sandbox. The #2926 `merge-tree --write-tree` caveat either does not apply to the seat or is resolved.
- AC-2936.3: With Codex unavailable, the round degrades as `onUnavailable` says, and the refusal or notice names the fallback. It never prints `Unexpected error`.
- AC-2936.4: The PR states the owner's consent (2026-09-26) and makes no change to templates or to consumer defaults.

AC-2936.2 and the live half of AC-2936.3 are proven by the orchestrator on the first real
Standard-tier round (premortem §2.6); the seat runs with `-C` on a temp dir and records its
envelope host-side, so the #2926 caveat does not apply to it (premortem §2.1 Q2).
