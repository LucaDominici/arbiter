# Plan: shell RED signature accepts indented `FAIL:` in runtime and emitted twins (#2898)

## Acceptance criteria (verbatim from the issue)

- AC-2898.1: The shell failure signature accepts leading horizontal whitespace (spaces or tabs) before `FAIL:`, and spaces or tabs between `FAIL:` and a non-empty label, identically in the runtime list (`src/evidence/tdd.ts`) and the emitted list (`src/templates/scripts/lib/_tdd-receipt.ejs`).
- AC-2898.2: The shell signature never matches across a newline: `FAIL:` followed by a line break, or by nothing but whitespace on its line, is refused.
- AC-2898.3: `FAIL-safe` prose, `FAILED:`, `FAIL:` in the middle of a line, and colon-less forms (`FAIL <label>`, `FAIL - <label>`, `FAIL (N failures)`) are refused.
- AC-2898.4: One parity test asserts that the runtime and emitted lists accept and refuse the same inputs, and that the failure identity of a column-0 `FAIL:` receipt is unchanged.
- AC-2898.5: When `record-red` finds no recognised failure signature, its refusal names the expected shell form `FAIL: <label>` (leading whitespace allowed, colon required) as the remedy.

## Non-goals

- `src/evidence/tdd-reexecute.ts` (GREEN `shellOutputFailure`) is owned by #2906, which will switch it to the shared shell pattern from `FAILURE_SIGNATURES`.
- ANSI stripping in the emitted list (runtime strips, template does not) stays as is.
- Colon-less `FAIL <label>` stays refused (owner decision).

## Approach

Executable brief: `.claude/plans/PREMORTEM_2898.md` (§1 manifest, §2 steps and regen order).

- RED: `__tests__/evidence/tdd-shell-signature-parity.test.ts` (runtime vs rendered `_tdd-receipt.ejs` over one accept/refuse corpus, plus the column-0 identity check).
- GREEN: one token in both twins, `^FAIL:` → `^[ \t]*FAIL:`, in `src/evidence/tdd.ts`, `_tdd-receipt.ejs` and its materialized hook copy; record-red refusal names the shell remedy.
- Regen: dogfood divergence pin, examples, bake goldens.
